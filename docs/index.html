    How it works

    Up and running in less than a minute.

    1. 1

      Download & Unzip

      Download Porta, unzip it, and place the Porta folder on a USB stick or anywhere on your computer.

    2. 2

      Open Porta

      On Windows, double-click porta.exe. On Linux, run START.sh. Porta opens right in your web browser.

    3. 3

      Choose Your Password

      Pick one strong master password to lock your vault (remember it well — it cannot be reset). Close the Porta window whenever you're done.
