    Everything you need on one stick

    Built to stay simple, private, and self-contained without cloud accounts or background services.

    People & Nested Folders

    Each person keeps their own nested folders, documents, credentials, and tasks. Organize fast with drag-and-drop, box select, Shift-click, and Ctrl+A.

    Strong Local Encryption

    Protected by one master password using Argon2id key derivation and AES-256-GCM encryption for your database and file blobs. Auto-locks after 10 idle minutes.

    Built-in Previews

    Open PDFs, images, and text files directly inside Porta — in the side panel or full screen with zoom and previous/next navigation.

    Phone Scanning to PDF

    Scan a QR code over local Wi-Fi, photograph pages, crop and flatten them, press and hold to reorder, preview Color, Gray, or B&W, and save as one PDF.

    Instant Search

    Press / anywhere in Porta to search across every person, folder, document, and credential at once.

    True USB Portability

    Zero installation and zero cloud. Back up by copying the Porta folder. Runs on Windows (porta.exe) and Linux (sh START.sh, even on noexec drives).

    How it works

    Start in under a minute from any USB drive or local folder.

    1. 1

      Download & Unzip

      Download the release archive and unzip it. Copy the Porta folder to a USB stick or anywhere you like.

    2. 2

      Launch Locally

      Windows: double-click porta.exe. Linux: open a terminal in the folder and run sh START.sh. Your browser opens http://127.0.0.1:8787.

    3. 3

      Unlock & Go

      Choose a master password — it cannot be recovered if lost. Encrypted data lives in Porta-Data next to the program. Close the console window to quit.
